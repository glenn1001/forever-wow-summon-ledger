# Glossary

Language of the summoning service sold in WoW: Forever for the Cozy Sleeping Bag quest chain.

## People

**Customer**
A player who buys the Package. Must be at least the Minimum level.
_Avoid_: client, buyer, pug

**Staff**
Everyone in the raid who delivers the service: the Advertiser and all Summoning teams. Staff sit in the Staff groups.

**Advertiser**
The Staff member who advertises the service, receives requests, invites Customers and Staff, and removes Customers after their final summon. May take payment.

**Summoner**
The warlock of a Summoning team. Casts the summon and may take payment.
_Avoid_: warlock (when the role is meant)

**Helper**
One of the two Staff members in a Summoning team who click the summoning portal.
_Avoid_: clicker

**Summoning team**
The three players, one Summoner and two Helpers, who summon Customers. Each has a Staff character parked at every Stop and relogs to the next Stop's character once the Batch has arrived.

**Staff character**
One character of a Staff member. Summoning team players have one per Stop, so the team has fifteen. All of them stay in the Staff groups, even while offline.
_Avoid_: alt

## Service

**Package**
What a Customer buys: a summon to every Stop, paid once. Paid either to the Advertiser before the first summon, or to the Summoner of the First Stop after arriving there.
_Avoid_: single summon

**Stop**
A location on the chain where Customers are summoned to. In order: Westfall, The Barrens, Stonetalon Mountains, Loch Modan, Thoradin's Wall.

**First Stop**
Westfall. Its summon is free and open to every Customer in the Waiting and Paid groups, including those already somewhere in Westfall. A Customer in Westfall who hasn't paid has arrived, and pays its Summoner. Every later summon needs the Package paid.

**Final Stop**
Thoradin's Wall. Arriving there completes the Package.

**Batch**
The Customers in the Paid groups, at most ten, who are summoned through all Stops together. The next Batch only starts once the previous one has arrived at the Final Stop.

**Minimum payment**
The least one Customer must trade to one Staff member for the Package to count as paid. Trades to different Staff members don't add up.

**Minimum level**
The lowest level a Customer can be to buy the Package, because the chain can't be started below it (14).

**Shift**
The period in which a Staff member has switched their addon on. Outside a Shift, nothing is automated and no trades are recorded.

**Waitlist**
Customers who asked for the service while the Waiting groups were full, in the order they asked. They aren't in the raid yet.
_Avoid_: queue

## Raid layout

**Waiting groups**
Raid groups 1 and 2. Customers who haven't paid yet.

**Paid groups**
Raid groups 3 and 4. Customers who have paid. They form the current Batch.

**Staff groups**
Raid groups 5 to 8, reserved for Staff.
